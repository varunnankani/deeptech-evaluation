The underwriting mechanism

The category is attractive only if field deployment compounds into reusable capability, better economics and eventually decision intelligence.

01 / CURRENT OPERATIONS

Services-first as distribution

Customers buy an inspection outcome rather than unfamiliar robotics capex.

02 / WHITE SPACE

Between NDT and robotics

Inspection expertise, certification, reliable robotics and industrial trust have to work together.

03 / WHY NOW

Validated budget, earlier automation curve

Octobotics already reports ₹2 Cr revenue and marquee customers, while the core workflow remains labour-heavy and hazardous.

04 / WHAT MUST BE TRUE

Reuse beats custom engineering

New verticals must reuse enough hardware, autonomy and software to prevent linear custom work.

05 / KILL CRITERION

Revenue cannot track headcount

If growth needs roughly proportional field teams and robots, the venture-scale case weakens.

INVESTMENT IMPLICATION

Gecko validates the ceiling but raises the urgency. Octobotics does not need to beat Gecko on global technical breadth today. Its near-term wedge is local deployment economics, Indian industrial relationships, field support and existing Indian Navy/PSU validation. The diligence question is whether those advantages harden into a moat before larger competitors expand.

Questions I would ask the founding team before investing

Each question should be capable of changing the investment decision, not simply filling an information gap.

01

Walk me through the last ten inspection jobs, including price, fully loaded field cost and contribution margin.

02

When you enter a new asset class, what percentage of hardware, autonomy and software is reused?

03

Show me the dated path to hazardous-area certification and the customer pipeline it unlocks.

04

How are inspections per robot and revenue per field engineer changing quarter by quarter?

05

What rights does Octobotics retain over inspection data, and what are customers actually willing to let you reuse?

06

Where does the model stop being a services business and start becoming an asset-intelligence platform?
